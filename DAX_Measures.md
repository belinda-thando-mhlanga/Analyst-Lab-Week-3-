# Superstore Analytics: DAX Measures & Calculated Metrics

This document outlines the custom Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) created in Power BI to conduct advanced analytics, develop Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), and investigate business profitability for the Superstore dataset.

## 1. Core Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
These foundational measures aggregate the raw data to provide a high-level executive overview of business performance.

**Total Sales**
Calculates the overall revenue generated by the business.

dax measures
Total Sales = SUM('Sample - Superstore'[Sales])

**Total Profit**
Calculates the net profit across all transactions.
Total Profit = SUM('Sample - Superstore'[Profit])

**Total Orders**
Counts the distinct number of orders (since a single Order ID can contain multiple line items).
Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT('Sample - Superstore'[Order ID])


**2. Advanced Diagnostic Measures**
These metrics were developed to investigate specific business problems, evaluate efficiency, and measure the impact of discounting strategies.

**Profit Margin (%)**
Evaluates the efficiency of sales by determining the percentage of revenue that converts to profit. The DIVIDE function is used to prevent division-by-zero errors.

Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)

**Average Sales per Order**
Measures the average transaction value.
Average Sales per Order = DIVIDE([Total Sales], [Total Orders], 0)


**Average Discount (%)**
Calculates the average discount rate applied to transactions, utilised heavily in identifying regional and product-level underperformance.
Average Discount = AVERAGE('Sample - Superstore'[Discount])

**Loss-Making Orders**
A critical metric used to identify the total number of unique orders that actively drained company profitability (net profit below $0).
Loss Making Orders = CALCULATE(DISTINCTCOUNT('Sample - Superstore'[Order ID]), 'Sample - Superstore'[Profit] < 0)

**3. Calculated Columns**
Used to categorise continuous data into discrete buckets for categorical analysis.

**Discount Brackets**
Segments transactions into four distinct discount tiers to analyse the specific threshold where discounting becomes unprofitable.

Discount Bracket = 
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),
    'Sample - Superstore'[Discount] = 0, "0% (No Discount)",
    'Sample - Superstore'[Discount] <= 0.20, "1% - 20%",
    'Sample - Superstore'[Discount] <= 0.50, "21% - 50%",
    "> 50%"
)
